Ashnimator • RideAlong

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 24 November 2026

1. Introduction

RideAlong is a university-focused ride-sharing application developed and operated by Ashnimator. This Privacy Policy explains what information RideAlong may collect, how it is used, how it is shared, and the choices available to users.

By using RideAlong, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use RideAlong, we may process the following information:

  • Account information: name, email address, phone number, profile/avatar information, and authentication information.
  • University or institutional information: information you provide for identity or institutional verification, including an uploaded identification document where the app requires it.
  • Ride information: manually entered pickup and destination locations, ride dates and times, ride requests, ride acceptance information, and ride history.
  • Vehicle information: information a driver provides about a vehicle, such as make/model and registration or plate details.
  • Notifications and technical information: information needed to deliver push notifications and maintain app reliability, such as notification tokens, device/app configuration information, and diagnostic or technical information processed by the services supporting RideAlong.

3. Location Information

RideAlong does not use your phone's GPS or continuously track your location.

Pickup and destination locations are entered manually by users. RideAlong does not request device location permission and does not collect continuous GPS movement data.

4. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • create and manage user accounts;
  • enable riders and drivers to request, accept, coordinate, and complete rides;
  • display relevant ride and vehicle information to participating users;
  • send ride-related push notifications and alerts;
  • maintain ride history and app functionality;
  • provide customer support and respond to requests;
  • protect the security and integrity of the service; and
  • diagnose technical problems and improve reliability.

5. Sharing of Information

RideAlong may display or share information with other RideAlong users when it is necessary to provide a ride-sharing feature. For example, information relevant to a ride may be shown to a driver or passenger involved in that ride.

We also use third-party infrastructure and service providers, including Google Firebase services, to support authentication, database storage, notifications, security, and app operations. These providers process information on behalf of the service according to their applicable terms and privacy practices.

We do not sell users' personal information.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. Data transmitted between the app and supported services is handled using appropriate security mechanisms provided by the underlying platforms and services.

No internet-based service can guarantee absolute security, so users should also take reasonable steps to protect their account credentials and devices.

7. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide RideAlong, maintain security, resolve disputes, meet legitimate operational needs, and comply with applicable legal obligations. When information is no longer required for these purposes, we will take reasonable steps to delete or de-identify it, subject to legitimate retention requirements.

9. Children's Privacy

RideAlong is designed for university users and is not directed toward children. We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so that we can review and take appropriate action.

10. Third-Party Services

RideAlong uses services provided by Google Firebase and related Google infrastructure. These services may process information necessary for authentication, data storage, notifications, analytics or diagnostics where enabled. Their processing is subject to their own terms and privacy policies.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when RideAlong's features, data practices, or legal requirements change. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised effective date. Users should periodically review this page for changes.

Request account and data deletion

You can use this page to ask Ashnimator Studios to delete your RideAlong account and associated personal data. You do not need to have the app installed to make a request.

How to submit your request

  1. Email ashnimator.studios@gmail.com.
  2. Use the subject line RideAlong Account and Data Deletion Request.
  3. Include the email address or phone number linked to your RideAlong account so we can identify it. Do not send your password or one-time verification codes.
  4. We may contact you to verify account ownership before processing the request.

Email a deletion request

What data is covered?

Your request covers your RideAlong account and associated personal data, such as profile details and ride-related information linked to your account, subject to applicable legal, security, fraud-prevention, and dispute-resolution retention requirements.

What happens next?

We will review the request, verify that it comes from the account holder, and contact you if additional information is needed. If deletion is approved, we will delete or de-identify the personal data associated with the account, except information we are required or permitted to retain for legitimate legal or security purposes.

Important: Sending an email submits a request; it does not itself instantly delete the account. Deletion is completed after identity verification and processing by the RideAlong team.
